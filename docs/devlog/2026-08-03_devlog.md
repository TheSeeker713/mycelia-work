## 12:15 — Kanban module, rev 4, and closing out Phase 1 for real this time

Turns out we weren't done with Phase 1 after all. Right after I called the
design "approved" yesterday I started thinking about how I actually plan
bigger work — Project A due in September, Project B also September but
lower priority, Project C sitting out in October — and none of that had a
home anywhere in the mockup. So we reopened it. Trello board mechanics,
Notion's habit of multiple views over one dataset, both at once: a status
board and a month timeline, same projects underneath, just regrouped.

Claude built it as a fifth pull-tab next to Tasks/Notes/Todos/Library —
compact read-only preview in the pocket view, full create/edit/drag-drop
behind "expand to full board," same pattern as everything else in this
thing. Card detail view, an inline form for linking tasks (glad that
didn't end up using a browser `prompt()` popup, would've looked out of
place next to everything else), and an AI panel with four real actions:
break into sub-tasks, suggest scheduling, tighten the description, or just
ask it something. Tested the scheduling suggestion by loading up September
with two projects first — it noticed and told me October looked more
realistic for the third one. That's the kind of small thing that actually
sells me on the AI panel being worth building for real later.

Hit a wall getting a visual on it, though. The browser tool's screenshot
compositing broke again, same failure mode as the dark-mode bug hunt a
few days back, so verification happened through raw DOM state instead —
clicking things, reading `getComputedStyle`, checking `projects.status`
after a simulated drop event. Slower, but it's the only way I trust myself
not to just eyeball something and call it fixed.

Sent me the artifact link and I looked at it and said flat out: this is
just notes and zen mode, same as before, where's the kanban. That could've
gone a bad way — but instead of insisting I must have missed it, Claude
went and did a completely fresh reload of the exact file it published, no
leftover test state, confirmed the Projects tab and the board really were
in there and rendering fine on a virgin load. So the file wasn't broken.
Asked me straight: did you actually click the tab labeled "Projects," can
you screenshot what you're seeing. I had — just hadn't registered which
tab was which. Found it, and yeah, it's good. Board and timeline both
work, cards drag between columns, the AI panel answers make sense for
what's actually on the board.

Phase 1 is closed for real now, Kanban included. Data layer's next.
